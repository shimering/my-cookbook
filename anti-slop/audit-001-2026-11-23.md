## Remediation Summary

### Tier 1: Hard Gate (Resolved)
- **Accessibility & WCAG 2.2 (#001–#012):** Added accessible names (`aria-label`) to all modal close buttons, search clear triggers, step reordering/removal buttons, catalog management triggers, grocery row action buttons, and week calendar pagination.
- **Touch Targets (#013–#015):** Expanded serving stepper controls in `RecipeDetailView` and `MealPlannerView` from 20×20px to 28–32px with full 44×44px interactive tap bounds. Increased recipe card favorite button padding to `p-2.5` (`touch-manipulation`).
- **Semantic Roles (#016):** Converted empty meal plan slot `<div>` into a fully accessible semantic `<button>` with focus rings.
- **TypeScript Safety & Hygiene (#017–#022):** Eliminated all unjustified `as any` casts in `cloudflareAuthService` and `cloudflareSyncService`. Replaced loose `: any` in `recipeExtractor` with Schema.org typed records and safe type guards. Standardized error catch clauses to `catch (err: unknown)` with error instance checking.

### Tier 2: Purpose-Gate (Resolved)
- **Visual Cadence (#023):** Added `aria-pressed`, distinct border styling, and active tactile press states (`active:scale-95`) to category filters in `RecipeFormModal`.
- **Purposeful Iconography (#024):** Replaced generic `<Sparkles>` in `GroceryListView` empty state with contextual `<ShoppingBag>` and `<Check>` icons.

### Tier 3: Quality Locks (Resolved)
- **Design Tokens (#025):** Replaced arbitrary pixel border radii (`rounded-[24px]`, `rounded-[20px]`, `rounded-[16px]`, `rounded-[12px]`) with standardized Tailwind tokens (`rounded-3xl`, `rounded-2xl`, `rounded-xl`, `rounded-lg`).
- **Tactile Feedback (#026):** Added active scale transitions (`active:scale-95`) across buttons, chips, and modal actions.
- **Render Purity & Compiler Rules (#027, #028):** Moved `filterSuggestions` to `src/utils/suggestionUtils.ts` to restore clean fast refresh. Removed synchronous state setting in `useEffect` for `AutocompleteInput`. Memoized `todayStr` in `MealPlannerView` with `useState` to maintain render purity.
